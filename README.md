# Pokémon Rockstar

A fan-made Pokémon Emerald-inspired adventure set in a neon-lit world of modded cities, underground music, and mythic creatures.

## Concept

Pokémon Rockstar reimagines the Hoenn region as a louder, brighter, more rebellious place where trainers travel through glowing streets, rooftop shows, and shadowy music scenes. The legendary energy of the region is tied to a forgotten pulse of sound and soul.

Features:
- Neon-town hubs and synthwave routes
- Custom story arcs inspired by alternative and rock culture
- Original characters and rival encounters
- A modernized Emerald-style region map
- Story beats featuring Lil Peep, Too Poor, and Ozzy Osbourne-inspired vibes

## Adventure pitch

You are a young trainer who arrives in a city where music is more than entertainment—it is a source of power. As you travel across the region, you uncover a hidden force awakening beneath the streets and in the ruins of old venues. Every gym leader and rival you meet has a different relationship to the music, the city, and the mystery behind the region's newest wave.

## Core themes

- Neon culture and underground rebellion
- Fame, identity, and creative freedom
- Friendship, pressure, and self-discovery
- A new adventure beyond the original Emerald journey
